# Lilt Charts

Work within this repository. Preserve user changes and designer audit evidence.

## Current direction (24 September 2026, updated 28 September for finance and range families)

V1 contains the retained chart families: Area, Line, vertical Bar, stacked Area and Bar, category Bar, Horizontal Bar, Stat cards (the `StatCard` with a sparkline, bars, meter, or ring; documented at `/charts/stat-cards`), Radial, Progress, Activity Ring, Combo, Funnel, Heatmap, Scatter, Sankey, Radar, Slope (slope and dumbbell), Range (floating and error bars), and Box plot. Finance families (Candlestick, Indicators, Price, Depth, Order book, Portfolio) ship from the `@lilt-ui/charts/finance` entry point and are a separate collapsible sidebar group. Series carry companion `fields` (which replaced `interval`) and `colorAt`; range marks such as `Candles`, `RangeBar`, `ErrorBar`, `BoxPlot` and `IntervalBand` share a private mark contract in `packages/charts/src/marks`. Chart pages are strictly about that chart, and every control has one home (5 October): Variants in the stage's carousel are the situations the chart handles (a target, a forecast, missing values, other data, a companion form), each in the look chosen for it, after the default card; Props in the stage's tab are the component's own props, so a form one prop makes (stacked, step, needles, pie) is a Props setting, not a variant; and everything that applies to every chart lives in the chart style notch, never on a chart page. There are no Looks. Lab (Customize) pages stay free showcases. What applies to every chart (colors, surfaces, backgrounds, 3D, axes, legends, hover, numbers, loading) lives under Customize at `/customize/*`, formerly Labs; a site-wide settings control applies palette, surface, axis, and depth to every example. The sidebar starts with Get started, then groups charts by job: Trends, Comparison, Part of a whole, KPIs, Patterns, and Cool (7 October: Skyline, Block city, Ridgeline, Spiral year — charts drawn in depth that other libraries do not offer; each is its own family with its own props rather than a variant of a plain chart, and Skyline and Block city share the prism grid in `packages/charts/src/cards/prism-grid.ts`). The Features sidebar group shows what charts do: Sync hover, Pin, Compare, Targets & forecasts, and Performance (a playground and benchmark measured in the reader's browser). New capabilities join Features rather than adding sidebar groups. Cartesian cards support linked hover (`sync`), `target`, and `forecast`; Line and Area marks draw long series from four points per pixel column (`decimate`, on by default) while everything else reads every row. Watch (client-side anomaly flags and a ranking board) was removed on 7 October: detecting unusual readings belongs on the server, and charts draw what it finds. The showcase gives each family an integrated Preview, Code, Data, Setup, and Customize experience. Bullet, Waterfall, Histogram, separate Examples/Templates galleries, the commerce template, and recipe generation/registry maintenance are out of scope and removed. Older assignment and product documents are historical where they conflict with this direction. See `docs/product.md`.

The package owns geometry, inspection, interaction state, comparison calculations, and accessible primitives. Consumers own their headers, legends, tooltip placement, and comparison JSX. Default legends use rounded color squares and can show current values. Inline dot legends and custom rendering remain available. Tooltips are opt-in. Preserve visible plot hover marks across chart families, strong color, loading/data truth, and reduced-motion behavior. Keep generated source aligned with previews.

## Observation additions (4 October 2026)

Treemap is a retained family under Part of a whole. Calendar heatmap, Timeline intervals, and Strip/beeswarm are companion forms under Heatmap, Range, and Scatter respectively, with their own key-typed card exports from the main entry point. Keep their flat/depth treatments and shared shimmer/draw/breathe loading lifecycle, including the skeleton opacity exit. See `docs/product.md`.

## Boundaries

- React/TypeScript library: `packages/charts`; Next.js documentation browser: `apps/showcase`.
- Package imports never reach into the showcase. No Next, Base UI, Tailwind, font, or app icon dependency in the package.
- The app retains the clean-shell floating sidebar, Manrope, one chart card, and Hugeicons Free. Mobile uses an accessible drawer.
- OpenAnalytics code is AGPL-3.0; implement Lilt's written specification independently. Do not copy its source, snippets, CSS, or assets.
- Do not publish, deploy, add a license, or claim package-name availability without a user request.

Use pnpm, functional React, strict TypeScript, named exports, kebab-case files, scoped `--lilt-*` CSS, and the existing Motion dependency. Prefer meaningful geometry, interaction, type, browser, and package-consumption checks. Report changed files, checks, limitations, and review evidence. The user permits breaking changes during development; migrate active consumers together.
